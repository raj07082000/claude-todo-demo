# Constitutional Amendments — Part 1: Shuruaat → Emergency

Har Amendment ke liye teen cheezein: **number ka hook**, **chain** (problem → kya kiya → result), aur **image description** (white background wali). Hook number se hi bana hai, taaki number dekhte hi yaad aa jaye ki amendment kis liye tha.

---

## Common style (har image ke liye)

- **Background:** plain white (#FFFFFF), flat vector illustration, thin dark-grey outlines, soft shadows.
- **Number:** top-left corner mein amendment number bahut bada, bold, **red (#E0262F)**. Uske neeche chhota grey text mein year.
- **Scene:** number ka hook scene ke beech mein ho. Number khud scene ka hissa ho, jaise number ke digits objects ban jaayein.
- **Labels:** sirf 2–4 words ke chhote black labels, pastel colour boxes (yellow, blue, green) mein.
- **Arrows:** chain left se right, grey arrows se: problem (dashed border box) → kaam → result (green border box).
- Har card mein ek hi rang ka accent ho, aur scene saaf, bina bheed ke ho.

## 1st CA (1951): 9th Schedule
- **Hook:** 1st = pehla bachcha, **9 mahine** → **9th Schedule**.
- **Chain:** courts land reform kanoon kaat rahe the → **31A, 31B** se bachao → kanoon ki tijori = **9th Schedule** → **15(4)** backward classes → result: **19(1)(a)** pe nayi restrictions.
- **Image:** Left mein ek judge kainchi se "Land Law" wala kaagaz kaat raha hai. Beech mein bada red "1" ek naye-janme bachche ke cradle jaisa hai, jiske upar "9" likha calendar latka hai. Right mein ek steel ki tijori (locker) hai, jispe "9th Schedule" likha hai aur andar "Land Laws" ke kaagaz safe rakhe hain. Neeche chhota mic pe "19(1)(a)" ka chhota taala hai.

## 7th CA (1956): States Reorganisation
- **Hook:** 7 = **saat phere** → States ki nayi shaadi, **bhasha** ke rishte se.
- **Chain:** bhasha andolan → States **linguistic basis** pe → **Part A/B/C/D khatam** → **14 States + 6 UTs** → do States ek **common HC (231)** → **258A** → result: **350A** mother tongue padhai, **350B** special officer.
- **Image:** Beech mein shaadi ka mandap hai, jiske chaaron taraf red "7" ke saat phere wala circle bana hai. Mandap mein India ke naqshe ke tukde joda bankar khade hain, har tukde pe speech bubble mein alag bhasha ka akshar hai. Side mein "A B C D" likhe purane dabbe toot ke gire hain. Ek chhoti building "Common HC" do states ke beech pul jaisi hai.

## 9th CA (1960): Berubari
- **Hook:** 9 = "**nau-do-gyarah**" → Berubari chala gaya.
- **Chain:** Berubari case: SC ne kaha zameen dene ke liye CA chahiye → 9th CA → result: Berubari **Pakistan ko transfer**.
- **Image:** Ek zameen ka chhota tukda (label "Berubari") pair laga ke bhaag raha hai, aur uske piche red "9" bhi daud raha hai. Raaste ke end pe green-white flag wala border gate hai. Upar ek judge ka hath "CA needed" board pakde hue hai.

## 15th CA (1963): HC judges retirement 60 → 62
- **Hook:** 15 → **1 + 5 = 6** → aage **2** lagao = **62**.
- **Chain:** HC judge 60 mein retire → 2 saal extra → result: **60 → 62**.
- **Image:** Ek boodha judge "60" likhe retirement gate pe khada hai. Red "1" aur "5" mil ke ek bada "6" ban rahe hain, aur uske saath ek chhota "2" jud ke "62" ban raha hai. Gate "62" pe shift ho gaya hai aur judge muskura ke wapas kursi pe baith raha hai.

## 21st CA (1967): Sindhi
- **Hook:** 21 ka **2** dikhta hai **S** jaisa → **Sindhi**.
- **Chain:** Sindhi bhasha list se bahar → result: **8th Schedule** mein (15th bhasha).
- **Image:** Bade red "21" ka "2" ek naag (S shape) jaisa mud ke "S" ban gaya hai. Uske paas ek nadi (Sindhu) beh rahi hai. Ek bookshelf hai jispe "8th Schedule" likha hai, aur ek nayi kitaab "Sindhi" shelf mein daali ja rahi hai.

## 24th CA (1971): Parliament amend kar sakti hai FRs bhi
- **Hook:** 24 = **24×7** power → Parliament kabhi bhi, kuch bhi amend kar sakti hai. 1971 ki tikdi: **24 Power → 25 Property → 26 Purse**.
- **Chain:** Golaknath 1967: FRs amend nahi ho sakte → 24th: **any part incl. FRs** → result: President ko **assent dena hi padega**.
- **Image:** Parliament building ke upar ek bada digital clock red mein "24×7" dikha raha hai. Building ke haath mein ek pen hai jo "Fundamental Rights" ki kitaab pe likh raha hai. Side mein President ek "Assent" stamp lagaate dikhte hain. Ek chhota gavel "Golaknath" bina taakat ke neeche gira hua hai.

## 25th CA (1971): Property pe kainchi, Art 31C
- **Hook:** 1971 tikdi ka 2nd **P** = **Property**.
- **Chain:** property compensation pe case → **right to property curtailed** → result: **Art 31C** (DPSP laws ko FR challenge se bachao).
- **Image:** Red "25" ke aage ek ghar hai, jiske paas paise ki thaili pe badi kainchi chal rahi hai. Ghar ke upar ek dhaal (shield) hai jispe "31C" likha hai aur andar "DPSP" ki kitaab hai.

## 26th CA (1971): Privy purses khatam
- **Hook:** 26 = **26 January** (Republic) → Republic mein raja ka khaas paisa kyun? → **Purse** khatam.
- **Chain:** rajaon ko privy purse → Republic mein sab barabar → result: **privy purses abolished**.
- **Image:** Ek raja crown pehne apni khaali thaili ulti kar raha hai, jisme se kuch nahi gir raha. Background mein "26 January" ka calendar page hai aur Indian tiranga. Thaili pe red cross hai.

## 35th / 36th CA (1974 / 75): Sikkim
- **Hook:** 35 **odd** = aadha (**associate** State); 36 **even** = **poora** State (22nd).
- **Chain:** Sikkim judna chahta tha → **35th**: associate State → result: **36th**: **22nd full State**.
- **Image:** Do pahaadon wala scene. Pehle pahaad pe "35" likha aadha bana ghar hai (sirf aadhi deewar). Doosre pahaad pe "36" likha poora ghar hai, upar tiranga aur "22" number plate.

## 39th CA (1975): Election disputes courts se bahar
- **Hook:** 39 → **3 + 9 = 12** → **12 June 1975**: Allahabad HC ne Indira ka election radd kiya.
- **Chain:** PM ka election radd → result: **President, VP, PM, Speaker** ke election disputes **courts se bahar**.
- **Image:** Calendar pe "12 June 1975" circle hai, jo red "3 + 9" se bana hai. Ek court ka hathoda ballot box pe girne wala hai. Uske upar ek glass ka dome hai jisme chaar kursiyaan hain (President, VP, PM, Speaker), aur hathoda glass se takra ke ruk gaya hai.

## 42nd CA (1976): Mini-Constitution
- **Hook:** 42 = "**Char-Do**" → chhota (mini) Samvidhan, emergency mein sab badla.
- **Chain:** Emergency → Preamble mein **Socialist, Secular, Integrity** → **Fundamental Duties (IVA)** → 5 subjects Concurrent mein: **education, forests, weights & measures, wild animals & birds, administration of justice** → DPSP **39A, 43A, 48A** → **tribunals 323A/B**, **131A, 139A** → result: **CoM advice President pe binding**.
- **Image:** Ek chhoti (mini) Constitution kitaab hai jiske cover pe red "42" hai. Kitaab ke chaaron taraf chhote icons orbit kar rahe hain: Preamble scroll pe 3 naye stickers, "Duties" checklist, school bag (education), ped (forest), taraazu (weights), sher aur chidiya (wild animals), hathoda (justice). Neeche President ke munh pe "CoM advice" wali patti hai. Background mein halka red "Emergency" siren.

## 43rd CA (1977): 42nd ka undo
- **Hook:** 43 = 42 + **1** → **ek kadam peeche** (undo).
- **Chain:** 42nd ki zyaadatiyan → Janata sarkar ne undo kiya → result: **131A repealed**.
- **Image:** Ek bada keyboard key "Ctrl + Z / Undo" dabaya ja raha hai, aur key pe red "43" likha hai. Saamne "42" wali kitaab ke kuch pages wapas ud ke bahar ja rahe hain, ek page pe "131A" aur red cross.

## 44th CA (1978): Emergency pe taala
- **Hook:** 44 = **4-4** → **double taala**: emergency pe + property FR se bahar.
- **Chain:** emergency ka galat use → "internal disturbance" → "**armed rebellion**" → sirf **Cabinet ki written advice** pe → **Art 20 & 21** kabhi suspend nahi → President advice **ek baar wapas** bhej sakta → result: property FR se bahar, **Art 300A**.
- **Image:** Ek "Emergency" lever pe do bade taale lage hain, dono pe red "4". Taalon ki chabi ek "Cabinet written advice" envelope mein hai. Side mein do dil (heart) "20" aur "21" dhaal ke peeche safe hain. Ek ghar FR wali building se bahar nikal ke "300A" likhe alag plot pe baitha hai.

## 45th CA (1980): +10 saal extension
- **Hook:** 45 → **4 + 5 = 9** → agla number **10** → **10 saal** extension.
- **Chain:** reservation ki time-limit khatam → result: **SC/ST reservation + Anglo-Indian nomination +10 saal**.
- **Image:** Ek ghanta (hourglass) jiski ret khatam hone wali hai. Ek haath red "4 + 5 = 9" ke baad "10" ka sticker laga ke hourglass palat raha hai. Neeche "SC/ST" aur "Anglo-Indian" wali kursiyaan.
